## Context
Ruang Main is an independently built browser gaming platform where people can play eight mini-games directly, without installing an app. Accounts are optional and unlock saved progress and solo rankings.

## Problem
Players needed a lightweight place to discover and play multiple games from any device, while the product still needed persistent data, rankings, and a reliable production deployment.

## What I built
- Created the game platform and responsive interface with React and Vite.
- Built backend services with ElysiaJS, used Supabase for account and game data, and integrated Google OAuth for sign-in.
- Delivered eight playable games: Kota Raya, Ular Tangga, Ludo, Arrow Puzzle, Fruit Merge, Tebas Buah, Blok Brak, and Botol Warna.
- Deployed the application on Cloudflare and connected the custom `ruangmain.web.id` domain.

## Outcome
Launched a public production platform that combines instant browser play with optional account-based saves and rankings.
