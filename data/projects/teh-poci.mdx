## The project

A finance and inventory application for Teh Poci outlet operations, with development documented from October 2026. This is an outlet-level operations project.

The starting point was three related Excel reports: **Cashflow, Inventory, and Rekap**. Daily sales, expenses, stock movements, and cash records needed to stay connected.

## What I built

- A daily recording workflow for sales, expenses, cash, and balance transfers.
- Recipe-based inventory usage, connecting product sales to ingredients and packaging.
- Outlet-specific products, recipes, and inventory, with editing and archiving.
- Opening balances, monthly carry-forward, historical price and recipe records, and conflict checks.
- Monitoring and Excel export that preserve the Cashflow, Inventory, and Rekap structure.

## Engineering approach

The frontend uses React, TypeScript, and Vite. A Cloudflare Worker handles the application API, with Supabase and PostgreSQL for persistence. ExcelJS supports workbook reporting.

The application keeps each outlet's data separate and preserves historical records when master data changes. Draft handling and revision checks help protect daily input during editing.

## Outcome

Combined daily finance and inventory recording in one workflow while retaining the reporting structure already used by the outlet. The current implementation focuses on outlet recording and monitoring.
