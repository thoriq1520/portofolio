## Context
This application focused on workshop operations and inventory management in the automotive domain.

## Problem
The product needed responsive frontend flows that matched provided UI/UX designs and integrated cleanly with backend services.

## What I did
- Implemented pages in React.js 18 and Next.js.
- Used Material UI, Axios, Formik, Yup, and Day.js where required by the project.
- Focused on inventory-related product workflows.

## Outcome
Delivered responsive pages and operational inventory features aligned with the design and service layer.
